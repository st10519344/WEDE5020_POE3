Craftsmanship & Sustainability

Discover our journey, our values, and our commitment to eco-friendly design.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 in Pretoria, South Africa, Aura Living Furniture was created to bridge the gap between high-end modern aesthetic design and responsible environmental practices.


Every piece of furniture we build is crafted by local artisans using 100% sustainably harvested timber, natural oil finishes, and non-toxic adhesives. We believe your living space should reflect elegance without compromising the health of our planet.

Our Mission

To design and deliver durable, eco-friendly handcrafted furniture that transforms homes into sustainable, serene living spaces.

Our Vision

To lead South Africa's sustainable home decor industry through innovation, ethical sourcing, and uncompromising craftsmanship.

Our Promise

Zero deforestation compromise—for every major timber piece crafted, we contribute directly to local reforestation projects.
